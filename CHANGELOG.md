# stampstudio

## 1.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 551ae6e: Templates: eight finished stamps that load a photograph and the plates designed around it, from a lighthouse in engraved blue to a full moon on a circle vignette. The pictures are Creative Commons, credited on the canvas and in the readme.

  Lettering now keeps its own paper. A window with a vignette reserves a band for the country line and the value instead of letting type fall over the picture, a loose value clears the frame rules, and the corner it sits in gives up its ornament.

## 1.0.0

### Major Changes

- First release. Print any artwork as a postage stamp and export it as a transparent PNG, GIF, MP4 or GLB.

  - Perforations punched from a signed distance field: gauge 7 to 16, adjustable hole size and torn fibre, plus wavy self-adhesive die-cuts, roulettes and imperforate edges
  - Paper rendered as a rough dielectric with laid fibre, an impressed watermark, toning, foxing blooms, handling creases and a gummed back the print shows through
  - Four presses, each re-separating the artwork: engraved, offset, photogravure and typeset, with intaglio relief that stands off the sheet
  - Stamp furniture on its own plate: frame styles, corner ornaments, a shaped vignette with a feathered edge, curved country line, corner value tablets and ribbon captions
  - Six bundled faces cut for stamp work, from Libre Baskerville to Pinyon Script
  - Duplex cancellation with killer bars and an editable circular datestamp
  - Eight presets, from the 1922 high values to a modern self-adhesive
